## What is Oryx?

Oryx is an action roguelite for PC. You pick one of four hero classes — **Warrior, Archer, Assassin, Mage** — and dive into a stylised dark fantasy world where every run is different.

You'll fight through enemy camps, capture outposts, clear trial totems, raid chests, and face down bosses. You'll buy stat-stick items from vendors. You'll draw a run-buff card every time you wipe an enemy camp. And when you die or extract, you'll come back stronger — every level grants permanent skill points that persist across runs.

**Inspirations:** Hades, Risk of Rain 2, Ravenswatch.

**Run length:** 20-40 minutes. Drop-in/drop-out co-op planned for the full release.

## The Core Loop

```
Pick class → Enter biome → Explore, fight, loot → Beat the boss →
Extract OR survive Wave Mode → Die or extract → Permanent skill points carry over → Next run stronger
```

Each run is procedural in encounter generation — same world, randomised vendors, randomised loot, randomised cards. No two runs play the same.

## Four Heroes, Four Identities

[Embed: 4-character lineup image]

- **Warrior** — Frontline melee. Durable. Reliable damage. The "I can take a hit" pick.
- **Archer** — Ranged precision. Distance is your defense. Position matters more than reflex.
- **Assassin** — Mobile burst. Strike, vanish, reposition. Glass cannon but slippery.
- **Mage** — AoE caster. Area control. Burst damage gated on cooldowns.

Each hero has **4 abilities** plus a **1 passive + 5 ability skill tree** unique to them. Hundreds of allocation combinations per class.

---

## Skill Trees That Stick

[Embed: skill tree UI screenshot]

Permanent progression matters. Every level grants:
- **1 passive skill point** (spend in the 20-node linear passive tree)
- **1 ability skill point per 2 levels** (spend in any of the 5 ability cluster trees, 10 nodes × 3 ranks each)

**Free respec anytime** — try different builds without grinding XP back.

Skill tree allocations persist across runs in your profile save. Playing a class only levels that class — Warrior runs don't bleed XP to your Mage progression.

## Items, Cards, and Chaos

[Embed: GIF of card pick screen → card selected → stat bar update]

Every camp you clear, you draw 3 cards — one from each category (**Strength / Magic / Agility**). Pick one. The buff is run-only and resets on death.

Every vendor stocks 6 random items at a stage-appropriate rarity weighting. Items are pure stat sticks — no equipment slots, just trade-off math. **32 unique items in Stage 1 alone.**

Chests scattered around the map are gold sinks. Open one for a luck-weighted rarity roll.

---

## The Boss Fights

[Embed: GIF of Mega Grizzlebark mid-telegraph windup → camera close to player]

Each stage ends with a boss arena. The Stage 1 boss — **Mega Grizzlebark** — has telegraphed heavy attacks (1-second windup with visual cue), follower spawns at every 30% HP threshold, and a fight that takes ~5 minutes when you're ready and 10+ when you're not.

Beat the boss, you choose: **Extract** to safety, or enter **Wave Mode** — endless escalating waves for XP only. Push your luck for level-ups, or take what you've got and walk.

## Risks and Challenges

Honest section. Required by Kickstarter, smart to write anyway.

- **Solo dev risk** — I'm doing the design + programming alone. If I burn out or get sick, timelines slip. **Mitigation:** all funding goes to outsource production, not to me personally — so the asset pipeline keeps moving even if I'm slow.
- **Scope creep** — Stretch goals can pull focus from the base game. **Mitigation:** Stretch goals are clearly post-1.0 or post-EA, never pre-1.0. Base game ships even if we hit no stretches.
- **Engine version drift** — UE5 updates can break things. **Mitigation:** Pinned to UE 5.7 for the duration of production. We'll upgrade only after 1.0 launch when content is locked.
- **Console certification** — Switch / Steam Deck certs are notoriously unpredictable. **Mitigation:** Stretch goal only. Default Windows-first plan absorbs the certification risk.
