# Subclass Concepts — 4 Classes × 2 Subclasses Each

Per D21 + D27: subclass unlocks auto at level 20. Steam EA ships **1 subclass per class**. Full EA ships **2 subclasses per class**. Each subclass adds **5 new skill trees** (= 5 new abilities + their modifier nodes).

This doc proposes subclass identities. Each is a 2-3 sentence brief + ability concept list + identity differentiator.

---

## Warrior

**Base class identity:** Frontline melee. Durable. Reliable damage. Sword weapon. Damage type bias: **Physical**.

### Subclass A — **Berserker** (Steam EA ship)

Glass-cannon variant. Trades durability for aggression. The "high damage, low survivability" branch.

**Identity:** Lean into damage. Lose armor as you hit harder. Wields a heavier two-handed sword visually.

**5 abilities (new tree set):**
1. **Frenzy** — Spammable burst attack. Each consecutive hit on same enemy increases damage by 5% (stacks to 10).
2. **Bloodrush** — Damage boost activates while at <50% HP. Lifesteal-adjacent effect (recovers 5% of damage dealt).
3. **Reckless Slam** — Heavy slam attack with knockback. Self-damage trade.
4. **Battle Cry** — Brief screen-wide buff (+30% damage for 5s). Single-target enemy gets marked for double damage.
5. **Last Stand** — Passive: when below 25% HP, gain massive AttackSpeed (+50%) and crit chance.

**Tree theme:** Damage scaling + crit + lifesteal + low-HP synergies.

### Subclass B — **Knight** (full EA ship)

Tank variant. Trades raw damage for defense + crowd control. The "shield wall" branch.

**Identity:** Defensive melee. Big armor stacks. Wields sword + shield visual (shield is purely cosmetic, no equipment slot).

**5 abilities:**
1. **Bastion Stance** — Activated buff: +50% Armor + +25% DR for 8s. Cannot dash during stance.
2. **Shield Bash** — Stuns target enemy + knocks back nearby. Refreshes if you're hit during cooldown.
3. **Taunt** — Forces all enemies within range to target you. Brief damage reduction taken.
4. **Guardian's Vow** — Passive: every hit you take below 100 damage is reduced to 50% damage.
5. **Iron Wall** — Become invulnerable for 2s. 30s cooldown.

**Tree theme:** Armor stacking + damage reduction + threat control + CC + survival.

## Archer

**Base class identity:** Ranged precision. Long-range, low durability. Bow weapon. Damage type bias: **Physical** (arrows) + status proc focus.

### Subclass A — **Hunter** (Steam EA ship)

Precision-focused. Trades AoE for single-target burst. The "elite-killer" branch.

**Identity:** Massive single-shot damage. Wields a longbow (visually larger than base). Cone aim + headshot crit reward.

**5 abilities:**
1. **Aimed Shot** — Long charge, massive damage on release. CritDamage scales aggressively with charge time.
2. **Crippling Shot** — Slows target enemy by 60% for 4s + applies bleed (Physical DoT).
3. **Stalker's Mark** — Mark a target. All damage dealt to marked target +50% for 8s.
4. **Vanish** — Brief invisibility. First attack from stealth = guaranteed crit.
5. **Bullseye** — Passive: every 5th shot is a guaranteed crit with +100% damage.

**Tree theme:** Single-target burst + crit stacking + stealth + marking + precision.

### Subclass B — **Ranger** (full EA ship)

AoE / multi-target variant. Trades single-target burst for area control. The "crowd-clearer" branch.

**Identity:** Multi-shot + AoE. Wields a shorter compound bow visually.

**5 abilities:**
1. **Volley** — Fan of 5 arrows. Spread radius scales with point investment.
2. **Rain of Arrows** — Target ground area, 3-second sustained arrow rain. Hits multiple enemies in zone.
3. **Trap** — Place ground trap. Triggers on enemy step, applies Poison DoT + slow.
4. **Hawk's Eye** — Brief expanded view + auto-target nearest enemy. Useful in chaos fights.
5. **Bramble Shot** — Shot bounces between 3 targets within 600cm.

**Tree theme:** Multi-target + area denial + status proc + trap-based control.

## Assassin

**Base class identity:** Mobile burst. Reposition + strike + vanish. Dagger weapon. Damage type bias: **Physical** + **Poison**.

### Subclass A — **Shadow** (Steam EA ship)

Stealth-focused. Trades raw damage for invisibility-driven burst. The "ambush" branch.

**Identity:** Stealth + backstab. Wields dual daggers visually (one in each hand).

**5 abilities:**
1. **Shadow Step** — Teleport behind target. Next attack = guaranteed crit + Backstab bonus (+100% damage).
2. **Smoke Bomb** — Brief AoE that grants stealth + slows enemies in zone.
3. **Death Mark** — Mark a target. If you kill the marked target within 8s, refund all ability cooldowns.
4. **Stalking Presence** — Passive: enemies don't aggro you until you're within 800cm OR you attack.
5. **Eviscerate** — Heavy single-target finisher. Damage scales with enemy missing HP (max 200% bonus at low HP).

**Tree theme:** Stealth + crit stacking + execute mechanics + cooldown refunds.

### Subclass B — **Acolyte** (full EA ship — name reuses cut class from D12)

Poison/curse hybrid. Trades pure burst for sustained damage-over-time control. The "dark magic" branch.

**Identity:** Poison + curse spreading. Wields curved twin curse-daggers. Visual: green-purple glow around weapons.

**5 abilities:**
1. **Toxic Cloud** — Poison AoE that persists 6s. Enemies entering or standing in zone gain Poison.
2. **Curse Mark** — Apply Curse DoT. Curse spreads to 1 enemy per second within 400cm of marked.
3. **Plague Strike** — Single attack applies Burn + Curse simultaneously (sets up Plague combo per D22).
4. **Withering Aura** — Passive aura that reduces nearby enemies' AttackSpeed by 25%.
5. **Soul Drain** — Channel: drain HP from target over 3s, heal yourself for half.

**Tree theme:** Status effect spread + DoT stacking + chain reaction synergy + sustained drain.

## Mage

**Base class identity:** AoE / burst caster. Cooldown-dependent. Staff weapon. Damage type bias: **Fire / Cold / Magic** elemental.

### Subclass A — **Elementalist** (Steam EA ship)

Elemental synergy focused. Trades cooldown reduction for raw elemental power. The "fire-vs-ice" branch.

**Identity:** Heavy elemental damage stacking. Multiple element types simultaneously. Wields a longer staff with floating elemental orb visual.

**5 abilities:**
1. **Fireball** — Single-target projectile, applies Burn.
2. **Frostbolt** — Single-target projectile, applies Chill.
3. **Elemental Convergence** — When you apply 2+ different elements to a target, gain +20% all elemental damage for 8s.
4. **Inferno** — Ground AoE that pulses Fire damage for 4s.
5. **Blizzard** — Ground AoE that pulses Cold damage for 4s. (Combo: cast both = guaranteed Shatter on enemies in overlap.)

**Tree theme:** Elemental damage stacking + chain reaction synergy + multi-school casting.

### Subclass B — **Stormcaller** (full EA ship)

Magic / Curse focused. Trades elemental variety for storm-themed burst. The "arcane glass cannon" branch.

**Identity:** Lightning + dark magic. Wields a crooked staff with arc effect. High CDR focus.

**5 abilities:**
1. **Lightning Bolt** — Instant cast hitscan damage. Magic damage type.
2. **Chain Lightning** — Bounces between 3 enemies, damage decays each bounce.
3. **Hex** — Apply Curse to target. Cursed enemies take +25% damage from all sources.
4. **Tempest** — Channel: AoE around player, pulses Magic damage every 0.5s for 5s.
5. **Mana Surge** — Passive: every Mana spend reduces all cooldowns by 0.1s. Stacks per cast.

**Tree theme:** Cooldown reduction + Magic + Curse + spell-chain synergy.

## Subclass authoring summary

| Class | Subclass A (Steam EA) | Subclass B (Full EA) |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | **Berserker** — glass cannon | **Knight** — tank |
| Archer | **Hunter** — single target | **Ranger** — AoE |
| Assassin | **Shadow** — stealth burst | **Acolyte** — DoT control |
| Mage | **Elementalist** — elemental synergy | **Stormcaller** — Magic/Curse spell-chain |

**Player decision framework at level 20:** "Do I want to play harder/faster (A) or with different game mechanics (B)?" Each pair gives a distinct identity within the same class.

**Authoring work per subclass:**
- 5 abilities → 5 ability skill trees
- ~10 nodes × 3 ranks each per tree
- Per subclass: ~3-4 hours authoring + ~1 hour playtesting
- Total: 8 subclasses × 4 hours = ~32 hours of post-EA content work

## Naming notes

- **"Acolyte"** intentionally reuses the cut class name from D12. Felt right for the dark-magic Assassin variant.
- **"Berserker"** also reuses (originally a future Warrior subclass concept from D12). Confirmed.
- All other names are flexible — if any conflict with established IP, swap freely.
